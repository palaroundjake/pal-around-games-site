Race to dress sloths too lazy to dress themselves!

Notify Me When It's Live

The alarm is ringing and school is about to start, but these sleepy student sloths are too lazy to dress themselves. Move quickly to outfit each sloth in a hat, shirt, pants and shoes matching their favorite color to score the most points and win!

Players

2–4

Time to Play

5 mins.

Ages

8+

Meet the Sleepyheads

They've Hit Snooze

But now they're ready to rise and shine. Are you?

Don't Be Alarmed

These sloths are lazy, leaving all their clothes in a pile. At the count of "1-2-3 Get Up!", each player wakes up their first sloth to begin the game.

Match Their Favorite Color

Every sloth has a favorite color. Search the messy pile for hats, shirts, pants, and shoes that match — but clothes are shuffled face down, so you won't know what you've got until you flip it.

Finish First

Finish dressing your sloths first and the golden backpack is yours. Just find it to claim your trophy.

Some Added Wrinkles

More Ways to Score

Slobby Sloth

Dress a sloth in all dirty clothes to score 16 points.

Goth Sloth

Dress a sloth in all black to double the points of black clothing.

Hamper

Collect the most dirty clothes and score 5 points. Or use it to stash clean clothes.
